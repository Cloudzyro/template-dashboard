## Audit (before)

Level: light to medium. The layout, data and app proportions were already right; the signs sat in the details.

- Greeting headline "Good morning, Maya" on Overview, and "Welcome back" on the sign-in screen
- Sparklines in all four KPI cards, and the change shown as a tinted pill
- Plan mix as a four-hue donut next to the main chart, so two charts competed
- Violet accent (#5b4bf0) for the chart, links, avatar and focus, unrelated to the lime logo; violet dot in the logo and favicon
- Sign-in panel: radial violet glow, concentric rings and a frosted glass card
- Uppercase tracked labels: sidebar "WORKSPACE" and "SCREENS", table headers on Orders and Order detail, "THIS MONTH" on sign-in
- 700 weights on page titles, figures, the logo, the sign-in title and the 404 title; 600 on most body labels
- Negative letter spacing on every number, including 14px table amounts
- Status shown as filled pills with a colored background
- Money in two formats on Order detail (`$1,068` next to `$85.44`)
- Buttons and inputs at 12px radius, same as cards; bars in the sign-up chart nearly pill-shaped (rx 7), and the bars as shades of the accent
- Nav rows 40px tall, section labels 28 above and 8 below
- Catch-all `transition` on buttons and rows; the switch knob animated `left`
- Trailing periods on short hints and switch descriptions
- Icon stroke 1.8 at 20px

## Direction

- Product: subscription analytics for a small SaaS team, showing recurring revenue, subscribers and orders
- Character: steady, exact, a little bright
- World: the monthly close, ledgers and billing cycles that repeat
- Moments: the revenue line, this year against last, ending on the current month
- Signature: the lime loop from the logo, used as the ring that marks the current month on the revenue line
